# City builder
A cool browser-based city building game where you manage roads, housing, jobs, resources, and city growth over time! This is actually really fun if you get the hang of it :)

## the features!

- Theres world generation with grass, water, and forest terrain
- Day and night cycle with weather effects, and the people don't work as much at night
- Population and job simulation with moving people
- Economy system with money, wood, and stone production
- Objectives and tutorial guidance for early progression
- Save and load buttons

## buildings

- Road (you need it to connect the city and build)
- House I (a standard house to hold people)
- House II (upgraded house tier 2)
- House III (upgraded house tier 3)
- Market (People can work here for money :))
- Lumber Mill (This generates wood)
- Quarry (Generates stone)
- Factory (Generates other materials)
- Small Park (boosts city's happiness level)

## tools

- Cursor (and you can click on placed buildings for the stats about it!)
- Road placement
- Building placement
- Demolish
- Save
- Load
- Settings
